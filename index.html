Payment & storage policy

Customers will be notified when their order is fully processed, frozen and ready for pickup. Orders must be paid in full at the time of pickup before products are released. Orders are expected to be picked up within 7 days of notification or being ready. A cold storage fee of $25.00 per day may be charged on orders not picked up within the 7-day pickup period. Orders remaining unclaimed for more than 45 days after notification may be considered abandoned and subject to disposal or other disposition at the discretion of DBR Custom Meats. Any outstanding processing, storage, disposal, or related fees shall remain the responsibility of the customer.
